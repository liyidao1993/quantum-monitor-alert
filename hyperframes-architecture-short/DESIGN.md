## Style Prompt

Vertical technical explainer with a clean blackboard-meets-product-demo feel: dark graphite canvas, crisp white Chinese type, cyan signal lines, amber highlights for tradeoffs, and small schematic blocks that behave like software architecture diagrams. Motion is brisk, readable, and tutorial-like rather than flashy.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#071014`
- Panel: `#102028`
- Text: `#F4F7F8`
- Muted text: `#9FB2BA`
- Cyan signal: `#27D7FF`
- Amber accent: `#FFC857`
- Green accent: `#51E3A4`
- Red accent: `#FF6B6B`

## Typography

- Primary: `Microsoft YaHei`, `Noto Sans SC`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Numeric and labels: `Consolas`, `Microsoft YaHei`, monospace

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use generic blue-purple gradients.
- Do not crowd the frame with paragraph text.
- Do not animate diagram elements so fast that relationships cannot be read.
- Do not use rounded oversized marketing cards.
